    It compares each account with itself, then applies Marcus's rules in order.

    Nothing is scored or weighted. Every verdict can be traced to one rule and one line of evidence.

    Step 1

    Clean the weeks

    Blank is missing, never zero. Outage weeks are thrown out for every account. They are found from clusters of P1 tickets on 10 or more accounts, not from a hard-coded date.

    Step 2

    Compare with its own level

    Δ =last 3 weeks − first 3 weeksfirst 3 weeks

    Averages of active vehicles. Flag a fall of more than 30%. Vehicles are what Larkspur prices on. Logins are shown beside them.

    Step 3

    Apply 13 rules in order

    The first rule that fits decides. Anything not settled with Marcus is an OPEN rule: the agent hands it back and names it instead of guessing.

    call this week

    A real drop, before or near the notice deadline.

    Goes to the account manager. Marcus too, if over $75,000 or an export request.
    ignore

    Seasonal, merged, re-keyed, outage, or flat.

    Goes to nobody. The reason still says why.
    ask Marcus

    A rule he hasn't settled yet, or data that makes no sense.

    Goes to Marcus, with the OPEN rule named.
    ask the owner

    The account manager holds the missing fact.

    Goes to the account manager, with the evidence.

    The agent never

    • contacts a customer
    • reads an email; it uses the date only
    • treats a blank week as zero
    • decides a hand-back case
    • calls an account "safe" when usage can't see the risk
    • applies the playbook's 20% login rule
    • writes to Salesforce or clears a flag
    • invents a rule
    • blames an account manager
